01 / THE PROBLEM

From a video frame to a motion signal.

A surgical instrument can be visible in a frame without its tip being precisely localized. This project aims to track a consistently defined tip across time, preserve the instrument’s identity, and estimate its displacement, velocity, and speed.

The intended outputs are annotated video and a CSV of tracking measurements. The current scope is image-plane kinematics: motion in the image includes the effects of camera movement and does not directly measure physical speed in mm/s.

02 / PROPOSED METHODS

Two paths to localization.

APPROACH A · REGION-BASED BASELINE

Estimate instrument-region motion

The team proposes testing pretrained ConvNeXtSAM on CholecSeg8k RGB sequences, then associating instrument regions across frames.

A bounding-box center is a motion proxy. Exact tip-location evaluation requires separate point annotations.

APPROACH B · KEYPOINT LOCALIZATION

Predict the tip directly

The team proposes fine-tuning YOLO Pose on EndoVis 2015 images with EndoVisPoseAnnotation labels to localize instrument landmarks, including jaw tips.

A consistent landmark definition and persistent instrument ID are needed before calculating motion.

03 / MOTION ESTIMATION

Position first.
Velocity follows.

Represent the selected tip as pᵢ = (xᵢ, yᵢ), measured from the upper-left corner of the image. For equally spaced, valid samples, a central difference estimates image-plane velocity:

vᵢ ≈ (pᵢ₊₁ − pᵢ₋₁) / (2Δt)
speedᵢ = ‖vᵢ‖
Position: px · Velocity and speed: px/s

Central differences use the next sample, so they suit offline processing or a delayed readout. The proposed pipelines also describe consecutive-frame differences. Use actual timestamps when timing is irregular; omit estimates when matches or tip positions are unreliable.

04 / EVALUATION PLAN

Make the uncertainty visible.

The README proposes held-out tip-location evaluation and explicit handling of occlusions and uncertain matches. It does not yet report validated accuracy or velocity results.

Evaluation questions and limitations documented in the project
QuestionWhat needs checking
Is the point the actual tip?Compare predictions with tip annotations on held-out frames.
Is motion linked to the same instrument?Preserve IDs, reject uncertain matches, and reset tracks between sequences.
Are velocity estimates meaningful?Verify timing and account for detection jitter and missing observations.
Can results transfer to another dataset?Validate separately; additional fine-tuning may be needed.
Does image speed equal physical speed?No. Metric calibration and depth information are needed for mm/s.
